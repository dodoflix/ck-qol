Client-side quality-of-life tweaks for Core Keeper, bundled as one mod. Everything is optional and configurable from the game's own menu, under Settings → QoL settings.

Auto Fishing

Casts and hooks for you, and stops baited spots depleting. Casting time sets how far the line lands, or Learn casting copies your own charge instead. Infinite fish shoal is host-side and does nothing on a server without the mod.

Auto Eat

Eats when hunger passes a threshold you choose, without changing the item you have selected. Picks the smallest food that helps, so a cooked dish is not spent on a small gap. Hotbar, main inventory and pouches can each be switched on or off.

Auto Summon

Keeps minions up as they expire or die. Three modes: the set you summoned by hand, the type you summoned last, or your cap split between the summoning weapons on your hotbar. Never summons past the cap, so a change of plan fills in as minions expire rather than culling healthy ones. Ctrl+R with a summoning weapon in hand toggles it for the session.

DPS Tracker

Your damage per second, under the minion counter, while you are dealing any. Detailed mode breaks it down by source, each with its icon. Burning and acid get a row of their own, because the game does not record who applied a condition.

Chest Search

Find an item in the containers around you without opening each one. Click the box beside the inventory, start typing, pick a name from the suggestions, and every nearby chest, station, pouch and dropped stack holding it is listed with a count. Each one is outlined in the world with the count floating over it, coloured by how much is in there. The search stays live once picked, so the containers keep being pointed at while you walk to them with the inventory closed.

Notes

  • Client-side only. Other players do not need it, and it changes nothing server-authoritative.
  • Nothing is enabled behind your back: every feature has an Enabled toggle, and settings are saved per profile.
  • Source, and an account of how it works, at github.com/dodoflix/ck-qol.
